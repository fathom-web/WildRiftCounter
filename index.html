How recommendations work

The recommendation engine uses role eligibility and a transparent composition heuristic (frontline, magic/physical damage balance, control and engage), plus lane-level win rate when the daily community dataset supports it. It does not claim to know matchup win rates without matchup data. Source data may represent China ranked play and may differ from your server or rank.
